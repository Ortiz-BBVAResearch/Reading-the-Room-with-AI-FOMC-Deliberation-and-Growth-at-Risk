# Exploratory figure 1: caption and placement draft

## Caption

**Predicted growth quantile shifts across signal terciles.** Each line reports the
mean difference between the challenger and its benchmark at a quantile of the
four-quarter-ahead growth distribution, across the 70 sealed forecast origins;
shading spans the interquartile range across origins and is descriptive, not a
confidence interval. Panel A compares M2+Hidden Dissent with M2, grouping origins
by the Hidden Dissent value available to M2+HD. Panel B compares M3 with M2,
grouping origins by NFCI. Panel C compares M3+Hidden Dissent with M3, again grouping
by available Hidden Dissent. Quantile shifts are measured in percentage points of
growth. The estimates use the pre-specified EQ-1B forecasts and penalties; no
forecasts are re-estimated for this figure.

## Main-text paragraph draft

Figure X shows how the predicted distribution changes across the information
states in which each signal enters. In the center quantiles (Q30–Q70), adding
Hidden Dissent to M2 raises predicted growth by about 0.14 percentage points on
average in the high-dissent tercile, while the average shift is −0.47 points in
the low-dissent tercile. The analogous shifts for M3+Hidden Dissent relative to
M3 are 0.26 and −0.42 points. Thus the all-origin exercise is consistent with a
small upward center shift in high-dissent states, alongside a larger downward
shift in low-dissent states; it does not show a uniform upward translation of the
whole distribution. NFCI produces a different pattern: its high tercile is
associated with a lower predicted distribution through much of the lower and
central range, while the uppermost quantile is volatile. The shaded bands describe
dispersion across forecast origins and do not establish statistical significance.

## Placement recommendation

## Supplement figure 2 caption draft

**Predicted growth distributions at selected forecast origins.** Each row reports
the 19 stored quantile forecasts (open circles) and their fitted ABG skew‑t
quantile function (solid curve) for the four sealed arms; the adjacent panel shows
the corresponding fitted density. Dashed lines mark realized four-quarter-ahead
growth. Event-window dates are selected as the origin with the highest available
Hidden Dissent value within each pre-specified window; 2008Q4 is added because
the GFC rule selects a different origin, and 2014Q4 is included as a calm-period
reference. Row headings report the largest RMSE across arms, calculated over the
19 stored quantiles. The GFC override has a maximum RMSE above 2.7 percentage
points, so its fitted curves should be read as a rough distributional summary;
the stored quantile points are shown to make that approximation visible.
